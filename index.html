About

Hi, I'm Renee

I'm the hands behind Fresh Prints. I opened this shop because I wanted somewhere that actually felt calm — not an assembly line, not a place where dogs sit in crates all afternoon waiting their turn. Somewhere I'd be happy leaving my own two.

That's [Dog 1] and [Dog 2] in the photo. They're the reason I got into this in the first place.

Here's how I work: one dog at a time. No cage dryers, no stacked appointments, no rushing a nervous pup through a bath because the next one's already waiting. If your dog needs a break, we take a break. If they've had a rough experience somewhere else, tell me — we'll go slow and build the trust back up.

Completed the American Kennel Club's Safety in the Salon training — salon safety, accident prevention, sanitation and handling special cases — and took the AKC Groomer's Oath to hold your dog's safety above everything else.

FAQ

Good questions

How much does it cost?

The Full Fresh is priced by your dog's weight: $40 for small dogs (20 lb and under), $50 for medium (21–40 lb) and $60 for big dogs (41 lb and up). Jazz's Pawdicure is $15 whatever your dog weighs. Heavily matted coats and dogs that need extra handling time may add to the total — we'll always tell you before we start.

What if I'm not sure which size my dog is?

Pick your best guess on the booking form. Renee confirms the size and the final price when she gets back to you, so a close guess is all we need.

How long does a groom take?

The Full Fresh runs 2–3 hours depending on size, coat and temperament. A pawdicure is usually done in 15–20 minutes.

What if my dog is matted?

We never brush out matting that would hurt your dog. If the coat is too tight to the skin, we'll shave it down safely and talk you through a plan to grow it back healthy.

Do you groom anxious or senior dogs?

Yes. We work one dog at a time, no cage drying, and we go at your dog's pace. Tell us in the notes and we'll plan extra time.

What's your cancellation policy?

Please give us at least 24 hours' notice. Repeated same-day cancellations or no-shows may require a deposit for future bookings.

Do you require vaccinations?

Yes — current rabies vaccination is required. Please bring proof to your first appointment.

What forms of payment do you take?

Cash, card, and major mobile payment apps. Payment is due at pickup.
